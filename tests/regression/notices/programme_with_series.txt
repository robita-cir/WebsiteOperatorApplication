The Example Robotics Summer Series is pleased to announce its next event, the Summer Programme on Mobile Robot Navigation. This two-week training programme is for final-year undergraduates and first-year research students. It begins on 15 July 2026 and ends on 26 July 2026. All sessions are held at the Training Hall, Example City. Participants will build and tune a small wheeled robot that finds its way through a maze. Applications close on 30 June 2026.
