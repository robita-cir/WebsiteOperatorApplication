Seminar announcement

Dr. Mira Anand from the Example Institute of Technology will give a seminar titled "Soft Grippers for Fruit Picking" on Tuesday, 12 May 2026 at 3 pm in Lab 3, Example City. She will describe how a gripper made of silicone fingers can lift ripe fruit without bruising it, and show video from field trials. Everyone is welcome. Tea will be served after the talk.
